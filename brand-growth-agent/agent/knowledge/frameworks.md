# Knowledge — Frameworks the Agent Uses

## F1. Meta Ads Delivery Stack (2024–2026)
| Layer | What it does | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Andromeda** (Dec 2024) | Retrieval: narrows tens of millions of ads to a few thousand candidates per person. Q3 2025 merged retrieval with early-stage ranking (Meta: +14% ads quality on Facebook). | Your creative's signals decide *who* it's even eligible for. |
| **Lattice** | Unified ranking model predicting conversion across surfaces. | Clean conversion signals (CAPI, value) improve ranking. |
| **GEM** | Large foundation "teacher" model distilling knowledge into retrieval/ranking. | System understands creative + user behavior better every quarter. |
| **Creative grouping** (Meta, Dec 2025) | Ads sharing key visual & thematic attributes are treated as variations of one creative and share learning/delivery. | 50 cosmetic variations ≈ 1 creative. Diversify concepts. |
| **The Creative Advantage** (Meta, Apr 2025) | "Focus has shifted from niche targeting to creative diversification." | Creative = targeting. |

**What counts as a genuinely different creative (diversity levers):**
1. Persona on screen (age, role, style, gender, number of people)
2. Desire / problem / Job-to-be-done
3. Category Entry Point (occasion, moment, season)
4. Visual world (home, street, office, studio, outdoors)
5. Format (UGC talking head, founder story, demo, street interview, static, carousel, meme, podcast clip)
6. Message frame (emotional, rational proof, social proof, comparison, education)
7. Opening hook type (question, bold claim, pattern interrupt, story start)

**Not real diversity:** color swaps, text-overlay changes, re-cuts of the same footage, same hero image with new headline.

## F2. Binet & Field — Long and Short of It
- Brand building = broad reach, emotional, memory-building, effects over months.
- Activation = targeted, rational, offer-led, effects in days.
- Average optimum ≈ 60% brand / 40% activation (varies by category & stage; Effectiveness in Context).
- Don't merge both jobs in one ad (Binet).
- Crisis adaptation (agent heuristic, not research): if runway < 8 weeks, start activation-heavy, then shift share toward brand each month as contribution margin stabilizes.

## F3. Ehrenberg-Bass — How Brands Grow
- **Mental availability** — being thought of in buying situations.
- **Physical availability** — being easy to buy (delivery, payment options, WhatsApp ordering, COD/InstaPay).
- **Category Entry Points (CEPs)** — map 10–20; prioritize by size × brand fit. Each top CEP = an angle.
- **Distinctive Brand Assets** — fame × uniqueness. Keep consistent across all creatives; branded early in video (first 3s).
- Growth comes mainly from reaching many light buyers → broad delivery suits this.

## F4. Jobs-to-be-Done / Switch Interview (Moesta & Spiek)
Forces of Progress:
- **Push** — frustration with current situation
- **Pull** — attraction of the new solution
- **Anxiety** — fear about the new solution (→ objections to handle in ads/offer)
- **Habit** — attachment to the old way (→ "switching cost" to reduce)
Timeline: First thought → Passive looking → Active looking → Deciding → Purchase → Consumption.

## F5. RFM Segmentation
Score each customer 1–5 on Recency, Frequency, Monetary.
| Segment | Rule of thumb | Play |
|---|---|---|
| VIP / Champions | R5 F4-5 M4-5 | Early access, co-creation, founder access, seed for value-based lookalike |
| Loyal | F≥3 | Tier perks, referral |
| Promising | Recent, 1 order, high AOV | 2nd-order flow within 30 days |
| One-time | 1 order, older | Win-back with new story/product, not just discount |
| At-risk | Was frequent, now silent | Personal outreach (WhatsApp) |
| Lost | Long inactive | Exclude from retargeting cost; sunset |

## F6. Emotional Relationship Segments (from the post)
- **Product lovers** → product drops, behind-the-scenes, usage education.
- **Founder lovers** → founder voice notes, journey updates, decisions shared early.
- **Community lovers** → events, member spotlights, co-creation.
- **Deal seekers** → keep them on bundles/offers; don't let them define the brand.

## F7. Brand Platform Canvas
Purpose (why we exist) · Tension/Enemy (what we fight) · Promise · Proof · Personality (3 adjectives + "we are X not Y") · Story Engine · Distinctive Assets · Tagline/Line.

## F8. Story Engines (if founder can't/won't front)
Founder story · Origin story · Brand character/mascot · Expert face · Community faces (UGC as heroes) · Craft/process · Mission/cause. Must be **true**.

## F9. Offer Engineering
- AOV: bundles, "complete the look/routine", free-shipping threshold, gift sets for CEPs.
- Anxiety reducers: guarantee, try-at-door, size exchange, WhatsApp pre-purchase chat, real reviews.
- RTO reducers (COD): order confirmation call/WhatsApp, small prepaid incentive (InstaPay/wallet), clear delivery ETA, fewer impulse-only hooks.

## F10. Measurement Stack
